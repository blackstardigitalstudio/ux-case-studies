# UX Case Studies — Blackstar Digital Studio

**A collection of UX audits, friction analysis and conversion optimisation work across real digital ecosystems.**

This repository documents how I approach digital product improvement: not from aesthetics, but from user behaviour, operational friction and business outcome.

---

## Methodology

Every case study follows the same structured process:

1. **Analyse** — observe flows, content, interfaces, CTAs, mobile experience and operational logic
2. **Audit** — identify errors, confusing points, unnecessary steps and barriers for the end user
3. **Optimise** — simplify journeys, priorities, visual hierarchy and conversion paths
4. **Document** — record what changed, why, and what the expected or measured impact is

## Case Studies

### 🍝 Il Raviolo — Restaurant UX & Booking Flow
**Sector:** Restaurant / Food · **Location:** Las Palmas de Gran Canaria

**Business problem:**
Italian restaurant in a tourist area with a website that didn't convert visits into reservations. Mobile experience was unclear, key information was buried, and the booking path had unnecessary friction.

**UX friction identified:**
- No visible CTA above the fold on mobile
- Menu buried under multiple taps
- Booking form too long and not mobile-optimised
- Hero didn't communicate the core value proposition immediately
- Local SEO not capturing tourist search intent

**Intervention:**
- Redesigned hero with single dominant CTA ("Reserve a table")
- Simplified navigation to 3 key sections: Menu / Reserve / Find Us
- Digital menu accessible in one tap
- Added local SEO signals and structured data for Las Palmas searches
- Reduced booking steps from 5 to 2

**Expected impact:**
- Reduced bounce rate on mobile
- Increased booking intent from tourists and locals
- Higher position in local search results

**Role:** UX analysis, conversion direction, digital system design, implementation support
**Stack:** Web, Local SEO, UX/UI
**Live:** [ilraviolo.es](https://ilraviolo.es)

### ⛽ Gas Project — Digital Studio Brand & Lead Generation
**Sector:** Digital Studio / B2B · **Location:** Italy

**Business problem:**
Digital studio without a strong online presence. Website wasn't generating leads or communicating credibility to potential clients.

**UX friction identified:**
- Generic positioning, indistinguishable from competitors
- No clear CTA for lead capture
- Portfolio not structured around outcomes
- No SEO strategy for studio-related queries

**Intervention:**
- Repositioned brand around results and specialisation
- Designed conversion-first homepage with clear value proposition
- Restructured portfolio as case studies (problem → solution → result)
- Added primary lead generation CTA with reduced friction

**Expected impact:**
- Increased qualified lead generation
- Stronger brand differentiation in a crowded market
- Better conversion from organic traffic

**Role:** Product strategy, brand direction, UX/UI, SEO
**Stack:** Web, Branding, SEO
**Live:** [gasproject.it](https://gasproject.it)

### 🎡 Mundo Imposible — Tourism Conversion UX
**Sector:** Tourism / Entertainment · **Location:** Las Palmas de Gran Canaria

**Business problem:**
New tourist attraction needing to quickly explain its concept to families and tourists who had never heard of it, and convert them from curious to booking.

**UX friction identified:**
- No immediate explanation of what the experience is
- Complex concept not communicated in 3 seconds
- Booking path had decision friction
- Mobile experience not prioritised despite tourist audience

**Intervention:**
- Designed visual-first hero that explains the concept immediately
- Simplified microcopy and removed jargon
- Fast booking path: interest → trust → action in under 60 seconds
- Mobile-first design prioritised throughout

**Expected impact:**
- Higher first-visit-to-booking conversion rate
- Reduced bounce rate from unclear value proposition
- Better engagement from family and tourist segments

**Role:** UX analysis, conversion direction, mobile UX, microcopy
**Stack:** Web, Conversion UX, Mobile
**Live:** [mundoimposible.es](https://mundoimposible.es)

        ## Case studies

        ### Il Raviolo (ilraviolo.es) -- Ristorazione
