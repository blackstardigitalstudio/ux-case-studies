# Il Raviolo — Restaurant UX & Booking Flow Optimisation

**Sector:** Restaurant / Italian cuisine
**Location:** Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Spain
**URL:** [ilraviolo.es](https://ilraviolo.es)
**My role:** UX analysis · conversion direction · local SEO · digital system · implementation support

---

## Context

Il Raviolo is an authentic Italian restaurant in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, serving a mixed audience of local residents and international tourists. The restaurant had an established reputation but a website that wasn't converting visits into actual reservations.

## Problem

The existing digital presence had multiple friction points that were costing the restaurant real bookings every day:

- **Mobile experience not optimised:** Over 70% of visits came from smartphones, but the experience was designed for desktop
- **No clear booking path:** Users had to scroll through multiple sections to find how to make a reservation
- **Menu not immediately accessible:** The menu (a key decision-making tool for restaurants) was buried under unnecessary layers
- **Hero didn't communicate value:** The homepage didn't immediately answer "what kind of restaurant is this?"
- **Local SEO not capturing intent:** Missing from key tourist search queries for Italian restaurants in Las Palmas

## UX Friction Identified

| Friction point | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| No visible CTA above fold on mobile | High — users leaving before scrolling | P0 |
| Booking path: 5+ steps | High — abandonment at each step | P0 |
| Menu not accessible in 1 tap | Medium — decision delay | P1 |
| No value proposition in hero | Medium — unclear brand positioning | P1 |
| Missing local SEO signals | Medium — lost tourist traffic | P1 |

## Intervention

**UX restructuring:**
- Redesigned hero with a single dominant CTA: "Book your table" — visible above fold on mobile
- Reduced navigation to 3 core sections: Menu / Reserve / Find Us
- Digital menu accessible in one tap from any page
- Booking flow reduced from 5 steps to 2 (date + party size → contact details → confirm)

**Content & positioning:**
- Clarified value proposition: authentic Italian cuisine made with imported ingredients
- Added social proof elements (Google reviews, Instagram integration)
- Simplified copy to be immediately readable for non-Italian speakers (tourist audience)

**Local SEO:**
- Optimised Google Business Profile with correct categories, photos and service descriptions
- Added structured data for restaurant searches
- Targeted tourist search intent: "Italian restaurant Las Palmas", "pasta Las Palmas", "restaurant Gran Canaria"

## Expected Impact

- Reduced bounce rate on mobile (clearer first impression)
- Increased booking conversion (fewer steps, clearer path)
- Higher visibility in local and tourist search results
- Reduced "how do I book?" enquiries via WhatsApp (self-service)

## What this demonstrates

> The ability to analyse a real business problem from the user's perspective, identify the specific points where conversion breaks, and restructure the experience around what the user actually needs to decide and act.
