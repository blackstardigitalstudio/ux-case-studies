# Mundo Imposible — Tourism Conversion UX

**Sector:** Tourism / Family entertainment
**Location:** Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Spain
**URL:** [mundoimposible.es](https://mundoimposible.es)
**My role:** Conversion UX · mobile-first design · microcopy · booking flow

---

## Context

Mundo Imposible is a new tourist attraction in Las Palmas — an entertainment experience for families and groups. Being new, they faced a specific challenge: nobody had heard of them, so the website needed to explain the concept, build trust and convert visitors to bookings in a single visit.

## Problem

New experiences have a specific conversion problem: the user doesn't know what they're buying until they understand it. Every second of confusion is a lost booking.

- **Unknown product:** No brand recognition, no word of mouth yet
- **Complex concept to explain quickly:** Needed to communicate the experience without boring or confusing
- **Tourist audience on mobile:** International visitors researching on their phones, often with limited time
- **Decision friction:** Too many steps between "this looks interesting" and "I've booked"

## UX Friction Identified

| Friction point | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| No immediate concept explanation | High — visitors bounce before understanding | P0 |
| Booking path had 4+ decision steps | High — drop-off before confirmation | P0 |
| Copy too long / complex for tourist audience | Medium — cognitive overload | P1 |
| Hero didn't communicate fun/excitement | Medium — wrong emotional trigger | P1 |
| No trust signals (reviews, photos, reassurance) | Medium — hesitation at booking step | P1 |

## Intervention

**Hero and concept communication:**
- Designed visual-first hero that communicates the experience in under 3 seconds
- Used imagery and microcopy to answer "what is this?" immediately
- Added a one-line value proposition that works for families, groups and tourists

**Microcopy and trust:**
- Simplified all copy to be immediately readable for non-native Spanish/English speakers
- Added trust signals: review score, visitor count, safety information
- Clear pricing and availability information upfront (no surprises)

**Booking flow:**
- Reduced decision steps: see → understand → price → book (4 steps maximum)
- Added a "book now, pay at the door" option to reduce commitment friction
- Mobile-first checkout: optimised for one-handed use on smartphone

**Mobile experience:**
- Touch targets sized correctly for mobile (minimum 44px)
- Key CTAs always visible without scrolling
- Loading optimised for mobile connections

## Expected Impact

- Higher first-visit-to-booking conversion rate
- Reduced "what is it?" enquiries via WhatsApp (self-explanatory)
- Better engagement metrics from family and tourist segments
- Improved visibility for tourist-intent searches ("things to do Las Palmas", "actividades Gran Canaria")

## What this demonstrates

> The ability to design for a specific conversion scenario (new product, unknown brand, mobile tourist) — understanding that the UX problem is not just visual, but informational: giving the user everything they need to decide, in the right order, in the least number of steps.
