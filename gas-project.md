# Gas Project — Digital Studio Brand Positioning & Lead Generation System

**Sector:** Digital Studio / B2B services
**Location:** Italy
**URL:** [gasproject.it](https://gasproject.it)
**My role:** Product strategy · brand direction · UX/UI · SEO · lead generation system

---

## Context

Gas Project is a digital studio offering web design, branding and digital marketing services. They had operational capacity and real results, but a digital presence that didn't communicate their value or generate inbound leads.

## Problem

The core issue wasn't the quality of the work — it was that the website didn't differentiate them from hundreds of other studios and gave no reason for a prospect to take action.

- **Generic positioning:** The website read like every other digital agency ("we make beautiful websites, we're passionate, etc.")
- **No lead capture mechanism:** The only CTA was a generic contact form at the bottom of the page
- **Portfolio without context:** Projects were shown as images with no explanation of the business problem solved
- **No SEO strategy:** No content strategy, no keyword targeting, no local presence

## UX Friction Identified

| Friction point | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| No differentiated value proposition | High — no reason to choose them | P0 |
| Single, generic CTA at page bottom | High — missed conversion opportunities | P0 |
| Portfolio = images, no narrative | Medium — can't evaluate quality of thinking | P1 |
| No SEO structure | Medium — invisible to search intent | P1 |

## Intervention

**Positioning:**
- Defined a specific value proposition focused on results, not just deliverables
- Repositioned from "web agency" to "digital results studio"
- Identified the primary buyer persona (small-medium Italian businesses wanting online growth)

**Conversion system:**
- Added primary CTA ("Get a free audit") above the fold
- Added secondary CTA in every scroll section to capture intent at different stages
- Built a simple lead capture flow: interest → free audit → proposal

**Portfolio restructuring:**
- Converted image gallery into case study cards: problem → solution → result
- Added metrics and context to each project
- Made the "what we actually did" clear, not just "what it looks like"

**SEO foundation:**
- Keyword strategy around Italian B2B digital services
- Structured content hierarchy for search engines
- Google Business Profile setup for local discovery

## Expected Impact

- Increased qualified inbound leads (not just traffic)
- Stronger brand differentiation in a saturated market
- Portfolio that builds trust, not just showcases aesthetics
- Measurable reduction in "cold start" sales conversations (prospects arrive pre-informed)

## What this demonstrates

> The ability to apply product thinking to a B2B service business — treating the website not as a brochure but as a lead generation system with defined conversion paths, distinct stages and measurable outcomes.
